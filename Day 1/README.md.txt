# 📅 Day 1 — AI Business Strategy & Management Simulation

**Workshop:** Four-Day Immersive Workshop on Artificial Intelligence (20 November 2026)  
**Case Study:** FitMeal / FITMEALS Startup  

## 📝 Description
This folder contains my Day 1 workshop deliverable. The session focused on applying Artificial Intelligence and structured data frameworks (`Data → Diagnosis → Strategy → Execution → Measurement`) to diagnose and turn around a struggling healthy-meal delivery startup[cite: 5].

### Topics Covered in [`Day -1 PDF.pdf`](./Day%20-1%20PDF.pdf)
- **Business Turnaround & Waste Reduction:** Cutting daily food wastage from 30% to under 10% using FIFO inventory management and AI demand forecasting[cite: 3].
- **AI Manager Strategy:** Funnel optimization, customer segmentation, and tracking key metrics (CAC, AOV, CLV, and churn)[cite: 5, 6].
- **CEO Unit Economics & AI Stack:** Identifying loss-making SKUs and leveraging tools like ChatGPT, Salesforce Einstein, Power BI, HubSpot, and Google Analytics[cite: 7, 9].
- **CMO & COO Execution:** Targeted influencer/gym partnerships, customer retention loops, kitchen SOPs, and delivery optimization[cite: 11, 17].
- **Research Analyst Audit:** Evaluating declining sales hypotheses and defining required historical data for validation[cite: 12, 13, 16].
